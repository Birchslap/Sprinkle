# NPC Promotion

An NPC you created at a lower tier has become more significant to the campaign. A minor character became recurring. A major character became essential. They need expanded depth — but they already have a history with the player that you cannot contradict.

## The Foundational Rule

What has been established is canon. The player has seen this person, heard them speak, watched them act. They have formed impressions and expectations based on what you have already shown them. Promotion builds depth *beneath* the surface. It does not repaint the surface.

## The Process

### Audit

Retrieve the NPC's character entry and review everything that exists: their appearance as described, their voice as performed, their actions as taken, their relationships as formed, their mental triad, their distinguishing habit. Read any DM notes that reference them. This is the foundation. Nothing you generate may contradict it.

### Infer

What do the established facts imply? A merchant who gave the player a generous discount might be naturally kind — or might be currying favor — or might be offloading flawed goods. A guard who looked the other way might be corrupt, or sympathetic, or simply tired. Choose the interpretation that fits the facts and serves the story. Where multiple interpretations are equally valid, choose the one that creates the most interesting person.

### Expand

Generate only what the new tier requires that does not yet exist.

For Minor to Major: add goals, fears, beliefs, bonds, flaws, secrets, backstory, planned actions, interaction hooks. Deepen the physical description. Expand the voice with more specific speech patterns derived from the mental triad.

For Major to Party: generate full physical statistics — STR, DEX, CON — alongside a complete character sheet. Deepen the psychological profile with hopes, detailed backstory, advancement potential, and relationship history with the player. The contradictions step from generation applies here — find the tension in who they already are and make it explicit.

### Integrate

New material must grow from established material. If the NPC was nervous when first encountered, the backstory should explain why. If they offered help freely, their goals should make that generosity coherent. The player should feel, looking back, that the depth was always there — they simply had not seen it yet.

## After Promotion

Update the NPC's character entry to their new tier. The player sees continued characterization — a person they thought they knew revealing new layers. They do not see a system event.
